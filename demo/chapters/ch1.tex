\subsection{A display formula}

The Euler identity follows from the exponential series:
\begin{equation}
  e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
\end{equation}

Align environments work too:
\begin{align}
  \nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} &= \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0} \\
  \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} &= 0
\end{align}
